# Observability Fundamentals: Traces, Metrics, Logs, and Events

This section covers observability Fundamentals: Traces, Metrics, Logs, and Events. It develops telemetry Signals, Resource Identity And Evidence Limits through mechanisms, examples, prerequisites and failure reasoning.

Read the module goals, teaching and closing summary in order. YAML checks are indexed separately and appear after designated summaries. Larger domains can include intermediate checkpoints before the final review.

## Learning Outcomes

- Distinguish traces, metrics and logs by the questions they answer.
- Explain resource identity and signal correlation.
- Identify sampling, cardinality and collection gaps in evidence.
- Distinguish numerical series from individual event records.
- Design structured records with useful identity and timing.
- Correlate logs with metrics without confusing correlation with cause.
- Account for retention, sampling and sensitive data.

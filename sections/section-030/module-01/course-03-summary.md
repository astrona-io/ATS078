# Summary

Admission applies additional decisions to relevant authorized resource changes. Mutation and validation have different effects, and built-in, declarative and external mechanisms have different operating dependencies.

Effective enforcement depends on trusted implementation, matching scope, failure policy and exceptions. Protect those controls and verify their actual decisions while preserving application compatibility and appropriate runtime protections.

Pod Security Admission applies selected profile requirements through enforce, warn and audit modes. Their effects differ, and policy versions and exceptions contribute to the actual contract.

Namespace policy changes need controlled ownership and compatibility planning for future Pods. Admission checks configuration rather than repairing applications or replacing image assurance and business-level authorization.

# Supply Chain Trust And Verification

This module develops supply Chain Trust And Verification. Use the explanations to connect intended behavior with responsible components and observable evidence.

## Learning Goals

- Trace source, build, publication and deployment trust boundaries.
- Distinguish provenance, signatures, inventories and vulnerability findings.
- Relate admission decisions to exact artifact identity.
- Explain continued maintenance after an artifact is approved.

## Before You Start

Examples illustrate declared relationships and reasoning. No execution environment is required or claimed.

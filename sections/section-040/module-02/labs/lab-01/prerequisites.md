# Prerequisites

- The `astrona` command-line tool, installed and working (`astrona check` passes).
- A container engine that kind can use (Podman or Docker) with about 2 GiB of free memory.
- `kubectl` and `jq`, `openssl` and `base64`.
- You know the terms used in the task. The reading parts of this module explain them.

The lab starts a single-node kind cluster (Kubernetes 1.35). The task is on the next page.
